# 📊 Superstore Sales Dashboard — Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of **Week 4 of my Data Analytics Internship at AnalystLab Africa**.

The objective was to transform the Superstore dataset into an **interactive Power BI dashboard** that provides a clear overview of sales performance, profitability, regional performance, and category performance.

This project allowed me to strengthen my skills in **Data Visualization, KPI Analysis, Business Intelligence, and Data Storytelling**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Monitor key business KPIs
- Compare regional performance
- Analyze sales and profit by category
- Identify sales trends over time
- Create an interactive dashboard
- Extract actionable business insights
- Present findings through data storytelling

## 🔎 Key Insights

### 1. 📈 Sales Growth

Sales show a strong upward trend from **2015 to 2017**, indicating sustained business growth over the period analyzed.

### 2. 🌎 Regional Performance

The **West region** is the best-performing region in terms of both sales and profit.

This suggests that the region combines strong sales performance with good profitability.

### 3. ⚠️ Category Profitability

The **Furniture** category generates strong sales but has significantly lower profitability compared with Technology and Office Supplies.

Its profit margin is approximately **2.49%**, while Technology and Office Supplies achieve approximately **17%**.

The analysis also highlights the presence of **high discounts, reaching up to 70% in some cases**, which can significantly reduce profitability.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, I recommend:

1. **Review Furniture discount strategies** to protect profit margins.
2. **Analyze the success of the West region** and identify practices that could be replicated in other regions.
3. Monitor **Sales, Profit and Discounts together** rather than focusing only on revenue.
4. Continue supporting sales growth while ensuring that growth translates into sustainable profitability.
